Section 3: Policy Proposals

1. Dynamic Regional Data Tariff
Mandate operators to price data at ≤2% of district household income. Fund via USO. Based on ML feature: data_affordability_stress (3rd most important).
2. Mandatory Tower Sharing
Require passive sharing in RED districts with <5 towers/100km². Reduces CAPEX ~40%. Based on: tower_per_million (9th feature).
3. Digital Onboarding via Safety Net
Bundle SIM+2GB/month with EGPP disbursement. Adds ~3.2M users at near-zero cost. Based on: poverty_gap_proxy (7th feature).
4. Union Digital Centre WiFi
Upgrade all 4,571 UDCs with public WiFi. Priority: RED upazilas with internet <40%. Based on: internet_access_pct (6th feature).
